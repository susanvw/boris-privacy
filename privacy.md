# Boris the Annihilator privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 October 2026. Contact: [support@svwdesign.com](mailto:support@svwdesign.com).*

Boris the Annihilator is a fitness app. It is built to keep your information on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where
Everything you enter or sync (personal records, profile, weight and other body measurements, blood pressure, cholesterol results, trends and settings) is stored **only on your device**, in the app's own storage. We do not run a server. We have no accounts, and we never receive your data.

## Health data
If you switch on "Sync with the Health app", Boris the Annihilator asks permission to **read** the kinds of data you choose from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android): weight, body fat, waist, resting heart rate, VO2 max, blood pressure, height, steps, sleep, heart rate variability and running workouts. It uses them to fill in your readings, trends and personal records. This data:
- stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else;
- is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold;
- is never written back to the Health app or Health Connect;
- can be switched off at any time in the app (Settings, Health app) or in your phone's health permissions.

## Exporting your PRs
If you choose Export PRs, the app writes your personal records to a text file and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you send it (for example Files, Google Drive or an email). We never receive it.

## Photos and camera
If you choose a pilot photo, the app uses your photo library or camera. The photo is turned into a small pixel image stored on your device. The original is not kept or sent anywhere.

## App lock
If you turn on the lock, the app asks for your Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. That check is done by your phone; the app never sees your biometric data.

## Reminders (optional)
If you switch on reminders, the app asks permission to show notifications. They are scheduled on your phone, at most one a week, and only say that a retest or health check is due. They never include your readings. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and the app does not use push notification servers.

## Tips (optional)
If the tip jar is available and you leave a tip, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and by our payment service RevenueCat, which receives the purchase and an anonymous identifier so the purchase can be processed. It does not receive your health or training data. Tips never unlock anything. The tip jar only contacts RevenueCat when you open it.

## What we do not do
No advertising. No analytics or tracking of you. No selling or sharing of data. No accounts.

## Your choices
You can delete everything by deleting the app. You can remove health permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

## Not medical advice
Boris the Annihilator shows general fitness information and reference ranges from published sources. It does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a doctor.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date. Questions: [support@svwdesign.com](mailto:support@svwdesign.com).
